How IOT is used

Discover how IOT is used in everyday life

Smart Home

IoT allows people to control lights, appliances, and security systems using their smartphones or voice commands.


Healthcare

Connected devices that monitor health and send information to healthcare professionals.


Agriculture

Sensors that monitor soil moisture, weather, and crop conditions.


Transportation

Connected systems that track vehicles and provide traffic updates.


Smart Cities

Connected streetlights, waste systems, and security cameras.


Manufacturing

Sensors that monitor machines and help improve factory efficiency.
